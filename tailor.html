Job-fit analysis

An AI feature I built: it reads a job description against my real experience and returns a structured fit report (matches with evidence, gaps stated plainly) plus a resume reordered for the role. See a finished example, or paste any job description.

The agent may only reword and reorder what's in his profile. It can't invent experience, and the output is checked against the profile before it's shown.
